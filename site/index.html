About the comparison

Metrics compare clipped predictions with clipped targets in the visible region, using the same bounds. MSE is divided by (maximum − minimum)²; RMSE is its square root. Pearson uses those same clipped values. Each supported bin pair is counted once, including the diagonal.

Choose Explore maps to browse all chromosomes, or Compare predictions to see the matching trained target, baseline and neural output. Overfit predictions cover 32 selected chr3/chr4 regions and their subdivisions. These regions were used for fitting. Here, the observed target and distance + DNase baseline match the selected model’s training target. Zero counts with positive expectation retain the model’s lower-bound label. Undefined expectations and unavailable inputs stay masked.

Elsewhere, the Viewer shows the original observed maps and the historical baseline in log-count units. A prediction is not invented for an untrained region or transform. Cross-transform baseline accuracy is not reported. Raw-reference colors do not affect comparison metrics.
